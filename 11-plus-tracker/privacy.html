1. Who we are

TutorsAreUs Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, is the data controller for personal data collected when you buy the 11+ Tracker. Contact us about anything in this notice at info@tutorsareus.co.uk.

We follow the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. What we collect

  • Order details: your name, email address, billing country or postcode, the amount paid and an order reference. Stripe collects these at checkout and shares them with us.
  • Card details: handled only by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Emails you send us: your message and email address.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider keeps short-term server logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and to keep the site working.

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies on these pages.

3. What stays on your device

The statuses and notes you add inside the Tracker are saved only in your own browser. They are never sent to us, and we cannot see them. Clearing your browser data will delete them.

4. Why we use your data

  • To take payment, deliver the Tracker and send your receipt. Legal basis: performance of a contract.
  • To answer your questions and handle refunds or complaints. Legal basis: performance of a contract and our legitimate interests.
  • To keep sales and tax records. Legal basis: legal obligation.
  • To protect the site from misuse. Legal basis: legitimate interests.

We do not send marketing emails unless you have separately agreed to receive them. We do not sell your data.

5. Who we share it with

  • Stripe, our payment provider, which processes payments and fraud checks.
  • Vercel, which hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the typefaces on these pages. Your browser requests the fonts from Google, which receives your IP address.
  • Our email provider, when you write to us.
  • Authorities such as HMRC, where the law requires it.

Some of these providers process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use safeguards recognised under UK law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK–US data bridge.

6. How long we keep it

We keep order and payment records for 6 years after the end of the financial year of your purchase, as required for tax purposes. We keep support emails for up to 2 years. Server logs are deleted automatically by our host, usually within 30 days.

7. Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive it in a portable format. Some rights have limits, for example we must keep tax records. Email us to make a request. We will reply within one month.

8. Complaints

Please contact us first so we can try to put things right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
